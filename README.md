# Structure of Matter Laboratory: Data Analysis

This repository contains the data analysis scripts developed for the "Structure of Matter" (Struttura della Materia) laboratory course. The analysis focuses on two main experimental setups: X-Ray Diffraction and the Hall Effect. The codes are designed to process raw experimental data, perform linear and non-linear fits, and extract fundamental physical quantities.

## 1. X-Ray Diffraction
**Context:**
This section contains the analysis of data acquired using a TEL-X-Ometer spectrometer equipped with a copper-anode X-ray tube and a Geiger-Müller counter. 

**Objectives:**
* **System Characterization:** Evaluate the Geiger-Müller counter plateau and analyze the X-ray emission rate as a function of the tube's acceleration voltage and filament current.
* **X-Ray Absorption:** Verify the Lambert-Beer law by studying the attenuation of the X-ray beam through Aluminum and Lead absorbers of varying thicknesses, allowing for the extraction of the linear absorption coefficients.
* **Spectrum Analysis:** Identify the continuous Bremsstrahlung spectrum and the characteristic $K_\alpha$ and $K_\beta$ peaks at different diffraction orders.
* **Interatomic Dimension Determination:** Apply Bragg's law to the diffraction data to calculate the lattice spacing of different cubic crystals (NaCl, KCl, RbCl).

## 2. Hall Effect
**Context:**
This section processes the electrical measurements conducted on a p-doped Germanium semiconductor sample, which is subjected to a variable magnetic field generated by an electromagnet.

**Objectives:**
* **Electromagnet Calibration:** Map the spatial uniformity of the magnetic field in the air gap and model the hysteresis curve of the electromagnet (magnetic field versus driving current).
* **Hall Voltage Measurement:** Analyze the transverse Hall voltage induced in the sample under two distinct configurations: constant magnetic field with varying injected current, and constant injected current with varying magnetic field.
* **Ohmic Drop Correction:** Perform linear fits on reference data acquired without a magnetic field to evaluate and subtract the Ohmic voltage drop caused by the slight misalignment of the transverse contacts.
* **Charge Carrier Properties:** Extract the Hall coefficient ($R_H$) from the processed data and use it, alongside the sample's measured electrical resistivity, to calculate the majority carrier concentration and mobility.
